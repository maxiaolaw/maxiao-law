Podcast
Stories from Nora and Hana
A podcast I created with my six-year-old, where we share stories and our love of books with the youngest of listeners. Each episode brings a favorite tale to life for pre-schoolers — a gentle invitation into reading, read aloud with the warmth of a bedtime story. Making it together has let me relive my own years as a radio and television host at the age of eight, now through parenthood.

Tool
Happy Moon
A Lonely Planet for little ones — made for young travelers who love to wander and want to know everything about where they are going. Happy Moon generates handy little guidebooks for aspiring readers, gathering stories and information about destinations, itineraries, and fun games to read and work on along the way. With the help of AI, parents can build and personalize the content, turning the hours on the road or in the air into part of the adventure.

Website
Poetic Names
A small interactive project for the chinamaxxing curious — it helps English speakers find a poetic Chinese name, each one drawn from the lines of classical Chinese poetry. Every name carries its own origin and meaning, while echoing the pronunciation of the individual's English name and their personal preferences.
